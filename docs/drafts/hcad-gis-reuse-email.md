Hello,

I am building a small open, non-commercial project called Hailprint. It maps radar-estimated hail size over Harris County and checks the map against public NOAA storm reports.

I downloaded the Parcels geodatabase from the HCAD GIS public data page. I would like to confirm how I may reuse it.

My planned use:

* I keep the parcel shapes and a short list of fields: account number, parcel ID, zip, city, year improved, stated area, and the stacked flag.
* I remove the owner name, mailing address, and situs address fields when I load the file. The project never stores or shows them.
* I publish derived results only, such as counts of parcels per hail-size band. I do not redistribute the parcel file itself.
* The project credits HCAD as the parcel source.

Could you confirm that this reuse is allowed under your terms? If there is a written license or attribution text you prefer, please send it and I will follow it.
